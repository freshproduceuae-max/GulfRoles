# 02 Brand and Content

Goal: collect the real words and the full offering — name, tagline, story, voice, every service/product, offers, testimonials — before layout.

Files:

- `content-intake-prompt.md`
- `content-inventory-template.md`

## Dependencies

- `docs/business/business-profile.md`

## Required Output

- `docs/brand/content-inventory.md`

## Success Criteria

- name, tagline, and story captured in the owner's real voice
- voice/tone agreed
- the FULL list of services/products/menu items is confirmed by the owner — nothing dropped
- offers, testimonials, FAQs captured if available
- CTA wording drafted and matched to the business

## Exit Criteria

- `docs/brand/content-inventory.md` exists and is populated
- the owner has explicitly confirmed the service/product list is complete
- progress tracker updated

- next phase is `03-assets-and-media`
- if new services appear later, mark this artifact stale and update it
