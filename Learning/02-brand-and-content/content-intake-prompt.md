# Content Intake Prompt

Paste this into the project AI when collecting brand and content.

```text
Your job is to collect the real content and the complete offering before any layout or code.

Before doing anything else:

- read `docs/business/business-profile.md`
- read `Small Business Websites/lessons-learned.md` (see "Keep the full offering")

Ask for and record:

- business name and how it should be written
- tagline / one-liner
- the story (how it started, who runs it, why to trust it) in the owner's own words
- the voice/tone (warm, premium, playful, no-nonsense)
- the COMPLETE list of services / products / menu items, grouped sensibly
  - read the list back and ask the owner to confirm nothing is missing
- prices or "from" prices if the owner wants them shown
- special offers or packages
- testimonials / reviews (with names/permission if used)
- common questions (FAQs)
- preferred call-to-action wording — suggest warm, service-hearted options and let the owner pick

Rules:

- never silently drop a service, product, or item; if a group is long, note it may become a dialog/accordion in phase 04, but keep every item
- keep the owner's real phrasing where possible
- do not invent claims, awards, or numbers

Then:

- create or update `docs/brand/content-inventory.md`
- update `docs/tracking/progress.md`
- record wording decisions in `docs/tracking/decision-log.md`
```
