# 01 Business Discovery

Goal: understand the business, who it serves, and what a successful website does for it — before content or design.

Files:

- `business-discovery-interview.md`
- `business-profile-template.md`

## Dependencies

- `docs/tracking/execution-preferences.md`

## Required Output

- `docs/business/business-profile.md`

## Success Criteria

- what the business does and who it serves is captured
- the primary goal of the site is captured (calls, WhatsApp messages, quote/booking requests, foot traffic)
- service area, hours, and contact channels are captured
- any seasonal, cultural, or community context is noted

## Exit Criteria

- `docs/business/business-profile.md` exists and is populated
- progress tracker updated

## Handoff

- next phase is `02-brand-and-content`
- if the business turns out to need bookings, payments, or accounts, note it as a trigger for later phases
