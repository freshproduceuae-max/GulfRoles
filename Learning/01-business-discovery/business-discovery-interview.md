# Business Discovery Interview

Paste this into the project AI at the start of business discovery.

```text
Your job is to interview the owner and understand the business before any content or design work.

Before doing anything else:

- read `Small Business Websites/canonical-paths.md`
- read `Small Business Websites/phase-dependency-map.md`
- read `docs/tracking/execution-preferences.md`
- read `docs/tracking/progress.md` if it exists

Do not design or write code yet.

Ask clearly, in plain language:

- what does the business do, in one sentence
- who are the customers (types, and what they care about)
- what should a visitor DO on the site (call, WhatsApp, request a quote, book, visit)
- what is the single most important action (the primary goal)
- what areas do you serve, and what are your hours
- how do customers reach you now (phone, WhatsApp, Instagram, walk-in)
- what makes you different or trusted (years, reviews, specialties, community roots)
- are there busy seasons or cultural/community events that matter
- do you need anything beyond a brochure site later (online booking, payments, ordering)

Then:

- create or update `docs/business/business-profile.md`
- update `docs/tracking/progress.md`
- record material decisions in `docs/tracking/decision-log.md`
- flag any need for booking/payments/accounts as a trigger for later phases
```
