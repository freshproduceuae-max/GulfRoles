# Changelog

All notable changes to the `Small Business Websites` planning template are recorded here.

## v0.1.1 - 2026-07-08

- **Mobile Page Architecture section added to `lessons-learned.md`:** 8 specific mobile design patterns from Blessed Banquets build: separate mobile.html with auto-redirect (phones ↔ desktops), banner-on-top layout, logo inside menu overlay, single-line menu items (no sub-descriptions), scroll-spy highlighting in menu (not distracting header labels), full-screen sheet dialogs, 2-column compact card grids, horizontal swipe strips, body-scroll locking during overlays.

## v0.1.0 - 2026-07-08

- Initial release of the Small Business Websites planning skeleton.
- Nine phases: 00 Operating Model, 01 Business Discovery, 02 Brand and Content, 03 Assets and Media, 04 Site Architecture, 05 Build and Implementation, 06 Conversion and Integrations, 07 Deployment and Launch, 08 Maintenance and Handoff.
- Top-level orientation files: START-HERE, README, canonical-paths, phase-dependency-map, session-resume-protocol, response-header-standard, COPY-INSTRUCTIONS, website-folder-scaffold, NEW-WEBSITE-PROJECT-CHECKLIST.
- `lessons-learned.md` capturing real build lessons (image/asset committing, case-sensitive paths, keeping node_modules out of git, implement-as-is, keep full offering, mobile-first, verify live).
- Reference-materials structure for images, brand assets, copy, inspiration, and links.
